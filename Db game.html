How to Play
- Move with A / D or Left / Right
- J = basic attack
- K = ki blast
- L = special attack
- T = transform
- R = restart

- Combo idea: J, J, K for burst damage
- More ki lets you transform and fight stronger
- Tournament mode uses multiple rounds and stage changes
